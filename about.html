About us

Preserving the legacy — Preservando el legado · Chappell Hill, Texas

Our seal

The Xoloitzcuintli is one of the world’s oldest companion breeds. Our seal pairs that heritage with Texas bluebonnets — a reminder that these dogs are raised here, at home, in Chappell Hill.

Established 2014

Chappell Hill Xolos is a home-based kennel dedicated to the Xoloitzcuintli in all its variety: toy, miniature, and standard; hairless and coated.

We believe the best start for a Xolo is a real household — daily life, gentle handling, and time — not a commercial production line. Families who join us do so by appointment, with room to ask questions and make a thoughtful decision.
