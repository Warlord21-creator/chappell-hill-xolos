A Texas home for an ancient breed

We raise Xoloitzcuintli as part of our household — not a warehouse, not a checkout cart. Families meet our dogs the old-fashioned way: inquire, visit, then go home together.

🏠

Raised at home

Our Xolos grow up in a real home environment in Chappell Hill, with daily handling, socialization, and the calm of rural Texas.

🐕

All sizes & coats

Toy, miniature, and standard — hairless and coated. We’ll help you understand which fit matches your lifestyle.

📅

By appointment

Visits Thursday through Sunday, by appointment only. No same-day placements and no shipping — we want the match to be right.

How placement works

This is not an online store. There are no listings with prices and no “add to cart.” When a puppy or adult may be a fit, we walk you through a thoughtful process.
